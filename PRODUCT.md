# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two equal audiences: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating candidates, and potential freelance clients looking to hire for web projects. Both arrive via links in job applications, LinkedIn, or referrals. They spend 60–90 seconds scanning before deciding whether to reach out. They need to feel confident immediately — that Abdullah is skilled, professional, and worth their time.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Abdullah Abu Sghaira (Abdoocoder), a full-stack developer and IT specialist based in Jordan. The site exists to convert visitors into inquiries — either a job interview or a freelance project. Success means a visitor lands, feels impressed within seconds, finds what they need (skills, projects, contact), and reaches out.

## Brand Personality

Energetic, vibrant, expressive. Abdullah's work spans systems analysis, React/Next.js development, and Firebase — he's technically capable and personally passionate. The portfolio should radiate that energy without shouting. Confident, not loud. Modern, not trendy.

## Anti-references

- Generic Bootstrap/template portfolio sites — formulaic, forgettable
- Corporate SaaS blue — safe, interchangeable, bland
- Over-animated / distracting interfaces — effects that compete with content
- Heavy "hacker terminal" dark aesthetics — overdone and impersonal

## Design Principles

1. **Show capability through craft** — the design itself is evidence of skill. Every detail — spacing, type scale, hover states — signals competence.
2. **Energy without noise** — vibrant and expressive, but never chaotic. Motion reinforces hierarchy, never competes with it.
3. **First impressions are everything** — the hero must establish who Abdullah is within 3 seconds. Name, role, personality, credibility.
4. **Bilingual by design** — Arabic and English receive equal visual treatment. RTL layouts are not afterthoughts.
5. **Contact is the goal** — every section builds toward an inquiry. CTA placement should feel earned, not pushed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 contrast for body text)
- Bilingual: English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) — both must be first-class
- `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (already implemented via framer-motion)
- Keyboard navigation support throughout
