## 2026-05-17 - LCP Optimization: Hero vs below-the-fold **Learning:** Aggressive use of `priority` on images that are likely below the fold (like in the About section) can negatively impact LCP by competing for bandwidth with the actual LCP assets in the Hero section. Additionally, loading heavy background videos on mobile, even if muted, consumes significant bandwidth and can delay the rendering of the primary LCP image. **Action:** Always audit `priority` props to ensure only truly above-the-fold assets use them. Implement mobile-specific logic to defer or skip heavy video assets in favor of optimized images to boost mobile LCP.
